New Vintage

Website Planning Document & Design Strategy

1. Site Name

Site Name: New Vintage

Reasoning: We are a brand that offers products with history, supporting the ecological movement and driven by a passion for items or clothing of historical or unique value.

2. Site Purpose

The New Vintage online store serves as a curated digital boutique for eco-conscious shoppers and vintage enthusiasts. The site provides a seamless e-commerce experience to browse, discover, and purchase authenticated vintage clothing, retro accessories, and historical unique decor items. Additionally, it educates visitors on sustainable fashion and the environmental benefits of upcycling and buying second-hand items.

3. Scenarios (User Questions)

  • What is the exact condition or history of a specific vintage clothing piece?
  • How does the sizing of vintage items compare to modern standard sizes?
  • What are the international shipping rates and delivery times?
  • What is the return or exchange policy for vintage purchases?
  • How can I track my online order once it has been shipped?

5. Typography

We use minimalist and elegant fonts that contrast beautifully to give a high-end editorial feel:

  • Heading Font: Cormorant Garamond (Serif, elegant, used for all major titles, headers, and brand emphasis).
  • Body Font: Montserrat (Sans-serif, clean, minimal, used for paragraphs, product descriptions, and navigation).

6. Wireframes

Standard e-commerce layout optimized for conversion and storytelling:

Mobile View Layout

  • Header: Hamburger menu, centered logo, search icon, shopping cart icon.
  • Hero Section: Full-width promotional banner with "Shop New Collection" Call-to-Action (CTA).
  • Featured Categories: Vertical scroll of curated collections (Clothing, Accessories, Home Decor).
  • Product Grid: Single-column item cards showing image, title, and price.
  • Footer: Newsletter signup, social links, and copyright.

Desktop View Layout

  • Header: Left-aligned logo, horizontal navigation links (Shop, Our Story, Sustainability, Contact), right search bar & cart badge.
  • Hero Section: Large widescreen banner with elegant typography and brand statement.
  • Featured Products: 4-column grid layout displaying high-resolution imagery and hover effects.
  • About Snippet: Two-column split highlighting the ecological and historical mission of the brand.
  • Footer: Multi-column footer with customer service links, payment badges, and newsletter subscription.
